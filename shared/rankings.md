# Scout ranking instructions

## Purpose and OA fit

Score how useful a finding is for Ocean Agentics (OA) to see now.
Use an integer from 0 to 100, not a percentile or sharing quota.
Return null only when usefulness cannot be assessed.

OA is an ocean nonprofit building open infrastructure and practical tools
for ocean governance, science and protection. Current work emphasizes BBNJ
(High Seas Treaty) implementation and its Clearing-House Mechanism (CHM):
treaty workflows, interoperable information systems and co-design with
delegations, scientists and communities.

Prioritize:
- CHM architecture, data standards, interoperability and implementation.
- Consequential BBNJ decisions and negotiating positions, including
  Secretariat/CHM hosting, governance, responsibilities and financing.
- Practical ocean-governance/data tools and useful partnerships.
- Substantial developments from collaborators such as Skylight; routine
  personnel or recognition news warrants less weight.
- Relevant resources and opportunities for OA or team members to contribute.

Other marine conservation, science, capacity-building and funding can be
valuable when the connection to OA's work is evidenced. Judge substance,
including advocacy, rather than format or institutional prominence.
An ocean/BBNJ label or familiar organization alone establishes no high score.

## Score

Choose a base, add an evidenced actionability bonus, and cap at 100.

| Base | Usefulness |
|---|---|
| 0 | No meaningful OA relevance. |
| 1–39 | Marginal awareness, promotion, repeated commentary or weak fit. |
| 40–74 | Substantive useful context with clear OA relevance. |
| 75–89 | Concrete, substantial value informing OA's work or a strong-fit opportunity. |
| 90–100 | Exceptional significance for OA's implementation environment, priorities or contribution. |

Important institutional or technical news can score 90–100 without an
available action.
New officially verified BBNJ ratifications have a base of at least 75.

Choose an integer bonus from 0 to 20 using these anchors:

| Bonus | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | None evidenced, closed, or known ineligible. |
| 5 | Useful attendance, tool testing or engagement. |
| 10 | Concrete, feasible contribution or collaboration with a participation route. |
| 20 | Strong-fit application, grant or proposal OA or a team member can realistically pursue. |

Check fit, eligibility, participation route and whether the opportunity is
open. Unknown eligibility prevents the full bonus. Urgency matters only
when a suitable action remains feasible. Awards to others can be useful
news but are not OA application opportunities.

If applying or attending was the principal value and is no longer possible,
give no bonus and score below 40 unless other concrete current value exists.
Judge outcomes, publications and recordings on their own usefulness.
Missing optional event times do not reduce usefulness; missing eligibility
or deadline evidence may limit the bonus. Do not invent facts or OA fit.
